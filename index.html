Our Philosophy

More Than a Haircut.
A Standard.

Ironside was built on one belief: that every man deserves a great barber. Not just someone with clippers — someone who listens, who cares, and who takes pride in their craft the same way you take pride in showing up every day.

We've been serving the Detroit community since 2011, and in that time we've built something we're genuinely proud of: a shop where regulars feel like family and first-timers leave as converts.

Walk-ins always welcome. Appointments respected on time, every time.

What to Expect When You Visit

01
Check In

Book ahead or walk in. Either way, you'll be greeted, offered a seat, and given a realistic wait time — no games.

02
Consult

Your barber talks first, cuts second. We ask questions. We listen. We won't assume we know what you want.

03
The Cut

No rush. We take our time. Clean tools, fresh cape, full attention on your head — not the clock.

04
Finish & Go

Hot towel, product if you want it, mirror check all around. You leave looking right. Simple as that.
